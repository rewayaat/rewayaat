Use these tips to get faster, cleaner results from the hadith search bar.
  • Use quotes for exact phrases, for example "pledge of allegiance".
  • Use field filters like book:"Al-Kafi" or chapter:"Prayer".
  • Combine terms to narrow results, for example book:"Al-Kafi" prayer intention.
  • Search in Arabic or English; both are supported.
  • Switch to Reading Mode when you want ordered navigation through a book.
