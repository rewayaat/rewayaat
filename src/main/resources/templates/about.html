About us

The Hadith Database is an open-source project, built in collaboration with the Academy for Learning Islam. We started it in 2017 with one goal: to make the hadith of the Ahl al-Bayt easy to search and read online, in Arabic and English, for anyone, anywhere. Its code is public on GitHub, where anyone can follow the work or contribute. Every narration and every narrator record is also open to developers through a free public API, and to AI assistants through our connector at rewayaat.info/mcp. The code on GitHub · See what's new · Contact us

The narrations are collected from translations of the primary sources, in print and online, and their contents and translations remain the responsibility of those sources. Some features, such as links between similar narrations, are generated with AI and may contain mistakes.
