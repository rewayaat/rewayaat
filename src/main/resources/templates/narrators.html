The men who carried these narrations, as the eight Rijal books have them — al-Najāshī, al-Ṭūsī's Fihrist and Rijāl, al-Kashshī, Ibn al-Ghaḍāʾirī, Jāmiʿ al-Ruwāt, al-Khoei and al-Māmaqānī. Each page shows what every book says of one man, and where they disagree.

Search by any name a book heads him under. A man is often headed differently in each book, and all those forms reach the same page.
