What does al-Kāfi say about patience?
Hadithsearch_hadith

Al-Kāfi has a chapter devoted to patience. Abu ʿAbdillah (a.s.) said:

الصَّبْرُ مِنَ الإيمَانِ بِمَنْزِلَةِ الرَّأْسِ مِنَ الْجَسَدِ

“Patience holds the same position in faith as the head does in the body. When the head is lost, the body perishes.”

Al-Kāfi, vol. 2 · Patience · #245
